# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Researchers, engineers, and technically fluent readers who want a fast map of recent LLM, RL, systems, safety, theory, and optimizer papers. They arrive looking for the argument structure, evidence quality, author relationships, and links across papers.

## Product Purpose

This site turns local paper-reading notes into a public research archive. Success means a reader can quickly understand why a paper matters, where the evidence is strong, what remains uncertain, and how it connects to adjacent work.

## Brand Personality

Careful, opinionated, traceable.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic docs themes, SaaS dashboard styling, hero metric templates, decorative cards, template-heavy tag grids, and vague marketing language. The archive should feel like a research desk with strong indexing, not a startup landing page.

## Design Principles

- Show provenance before polish: source links, version notes, and evidence boundaries stay visible.
- Make relationships scannable: paper-to-paper and author-to-author context should be easier to find than in a flat reading list.
- Keep the interface quiet but not anonymous: restraint should sharpen the archive's point of view.
- Favor dense clarity over decoration: every repeated element should help scanning, comparison, or navigation.
- Preserve long-form reading comfort: article pages should reduce chrome and keep the text column calm.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for all text and interactive states. Preserve keyboard focus indicators, avoid motion that gates content, respect reduced-motion preferences, and keep mobile tap targets usable.
